About Layoverly

Layoverly makes short, practical city guides for people with very little time: a weekend, a stopover, or a long layover.

Every guide is built around the same questions: how do I get in from the airport, what's actually worth my few hours, what will it cost, and where can I eat? Places are labelled honestly. If we can't confirm something, we say so.

Guides are researched against official sources and checked by people who fly to these cities regularly. Prices and opening times change, so each guide shows the date it was last checked.

# Layoverly content guide (for whoever publishes the weekly guide)

## Publishing a new city guide
1. Take the next city from the queue below that has no page in /guides/ yet.
2. Research it fresh on the web (current year): airport-to-city options with prices and times, the top sights with ticket prices and current opening hours, a realistic 24h route in walking order, a second-day plan, layover advice by hours available, typical costs, and where to eat including Muslim-friendly places.
3. Create /guides/<city-slug>.html by copying the structure of /guides/athens.html exactly (same header, sections, table styles, footer). Title format: "<City> in 24–48 Hours: Itinerary, Airport Transfer & Costs (<year>) | Layoverly". Add a meta description of under 160 characters and a canonical link.
4. Add a card for the city at the top of the list in /guides/index.html (below the GUIDE-CARDS comment) and replace the oldest card in the "Latest guides" section of /index.html, keeping at most 6 cards there.
5. Add the URL to sitemap.xml with today's date.
6. Commit with the message "Add <City> guide" and push to main.

## Rules
- Write in plain, original English. Never copy text from other sites.
- Every price and opening time must come from a current source. If sources disagree, give a range or say "check on the day".
- Food labels must be honest: "Advertised halal", "Partly halal: ask", or "Not confirmed". Never call a place halal without a source.
- No personal information about the site owner anywhere on the site. Never mention any airline employer.
- No affiliate links until the owner provides the affiliate IDs.
- Keep each page useful on a phone: short paragraphs and tables.
